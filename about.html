Built around people.

SautiLink is a social network built in Tanzania for people around the world. Share ideas, join conversations and build communities with clearer choices for your identity, privacy and expression.

Create an accountLog inExplore privacy at SautiLink

A space worthy of your voice.

Your conversations, photos and thoughts should remain yours. The platform should earn the privilege of carrying them.

SautiLink gives people a more direct relationship with social technology: publish, follow, discuss, message, create communities and manage privacy.

A useful social network needs some data to work. We aim to collect what the service needs, make important controls understandable and keep expression open while enforcing rules against serious abuse, fraud and harm.

Connect in your own way.

SautiLink is available now, with familiar ways to share and new experiences as the service evolves.

Posts & conversations

Share text, media and polls, join reply threads, follow people and interact with posts through likes, reposts and saves.

Rooms & communities

Create or join public and private Rooms with membership controls and local Owner, Admin and Moderator roles.

Messages & controls

Use direct messages, notifications, discovery, blocking, muting, reporting, appeals, exports and account-deletion controls.

Clear principles. Practical choices.

The ideas that guide the everyday choices we make.

Privacy first

We aim to minimise unnecessary collection, do not sell personal data, and provide practical audience, messaging and account controls.

Open expression

Criticism, journalism, debate, satire, art and peaceful disagreement belong on SautiLink when they follow the rules.

Built in Africa

Developed from Tanzania with an understanding of African users, the Global South and a connected world.
