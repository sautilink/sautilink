Our mission

Build a digital space worthy of your voice.

SautiLink exists to give people a more direct relationship with social technology: publish, follow, discuss, message, create communities and manage privacy without pretending that a useful social network can operate without any data at all.

We aim to collect what the service needs, make important controls understandable and keep expression open while enforcing rules against serious abuse, fraud and harm.

“Your voice belongs to you. The platform should earn the privilege of carrying it.”
SautiLink founding principle
SautiLink today

A social network, not a waiting room.

The service is live and continues to evolve.

Posts & conversations

Share text, media and polls, join reply threads, follow people and interact with posts through likes, reposts and saves.

Rooms & communities

Create or join public and private Rooms with membership controls and local Owner, Admin and Moderator roles.

Messages & controls

Use direct messages, notifications, discovery, blocking, muting, reporting, appeals, exports and account-deletion controls.

What guides us
