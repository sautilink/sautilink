Our purpose is to help readers understand the people, events and ideas that shape everyday life.

A story deserves attention to what happened, why it matters and who it affects. That means listening closely, asking useful questions and making room for perspectives that can be overlooked.

SautiNote’s approach puts evidence, thoughtful context and independent editorial judgement at the centre of the work.

The story matters.
So does how it’s told.

The principles that guide SautiNote’s reporting and storytelling.

Accuracy before speed.

Verification and useful context matter more than being first. Careful reporting starts with questions, evidence and attention to detail.

Independent judgement.

Editorial decisions should follow the evidence and the public interest. That principle gives the reporting its purpose.

People in context.

African perspectives, local knowledge and lived experience deserve the space to be understood in their own terms.

Finding SautiNote and reaching the right team.

Where can I read SautiNote?

Visit sautinote.com to explore the publication. The links on this page open the SautiNote website in a new tab.

How can I share a story tip?

Email note@sautinote.com with an outline of the story, why it matters and any useful background or source links.

Who should I contact about a correction?

Contact the editorial team at note@sautinote.com. Include the article link and explain the information you believe needs attention.

Where can I get help with my SautiLink account?

Visit the SautiLink Help Centre for account, sign-in and product support. For SautiNote reporting or editorial inquiries, use the editorial email above.
