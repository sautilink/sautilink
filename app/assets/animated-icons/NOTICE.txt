The JSON files are unmodified copies of icons/<filename>.json at the pinned
commit, with one filename alias: notification.json is icons/notification4.json
(the ringing bell, without a mute slash or an artificial unread badge).
They are raw Lottie animation data, not shadcn registry documents.
All animations use a 32 x 32 artboard and have no external assets. The official
catalogue marks every icon below requiresFullPlayer=false: the existing
self-hosted lottie_light SVG renderer can play them without another dependency.
https://useanimations.com/icons.json
https://useanimations.com/documentation#use-without-react

Playback notes, checked against the actual pinned animation data:
The op value is an exclusive end; a player's last displayed frame is op - 1.
Do not infer on/off state from the generic HTML example on an icon page.

notification.json has floating point op=60.0000024438501 and
fr=29.9700012207031. It settles to its initial rotation by frame 50 and has no
slash, white knockout or unread dot. Replay once when opening notifications;
do not use its upstream continuous-loop mode for a navigation control.

Toggle heart/bookmark forward to select and backward to clear. Microphone runs
forward to a slashed state, so its semantics are inverted relative to an
enabled/on boolean. Keep microphone at frame 0 for idle voice recording, then
show activity looping only while a recording is active; do not imply muting.
Replay icons play from frame 0 once;
home/settings play forward on hover/focus and backward on leave/blur. Replaying
searchToX would briefly replace search with close: use only for a real open/close
state, or keep its magnifier frame static for search navigation. Repeat's end
state is visually identical because its arrows have half-turn symmetry.

Color handling: black [0,0,0,1] strokes and fills are foreground and may follow
currentColor. The white [1,1,1,1] stroke in microphone is a
knockout behind the slash. Map it to the actual containing surface, never to
the foreground. Preserve fill='none'. Heart/bookmark animate fill opacity as
part of their selected state; do not override that opacity.

Accessibility: keep existing real button/link controls, names and handlers;
animations are decorative. For reduced motion use static state frames, skip
replays and jump toggles to their correct resting endpoint.
