How to install the official Android app

Install SautiLink securely through Google Play.

  1. Open the Google Play listing. Select “Get it on Google Play” above.
  2. Install SautiLink. Tap Install when the app is available for your device and region.
  3. Get started. Open SautiLink and sign in or create an account.

Security tip: use the official Google Play listing instead of unverified APKs or third-party download links.
