How to install SautiLink

Follow these steps after downloading the APK.

  1. Open the downloaded APK. Find it in your browser downloads or the Downloads folder on your device.
  2. Allow installation if prompted. If Android asks for permission, open Settings, enable “Allow from this source” for your browser or file manager, then return to the installer.
  3. Continue past the Play Protect notice. On the “App blocked to protect your device” popup, tap “More details”.
  4. Complete the installation. Tap “Install anyway”, wait for installation to finish, then tap “Open”.

For your safety: only continue past this notice when the APK was downloaded using the official button on sautilink.com. Do not disable Play Protect or install a SautiLink APK received from another source.
