What is SautiLink?

SautiLink is a privacy-first social network being built in Tanzania for people around the world. It is designed to give people clearer control over identity, data and expression.

When will SautiLink launch?

SautiLink is currently in pre-launch. We have not published a final launch date. Join the verified waitlist and we will contact you when access becomes available.

How does the waitlist work?

Choose an available username, enter your email and submit the eight-digit verification code we send. Your waitlist place is created only after the email is verified.

Is my waitlist username guaranteed forever?

The waitlist records your verified username choice for launch planning. Final account access remains subject to SautiLink's Terms, username rules, safety requirements and launch availability.

What information does the waitlist collect?

It collects your selected username, verified email address, waitlist status and limited technical information needed for security and abuse prevention. Read the Privacy Policy for details and deletion options.

How does SautiLink protect privacy?

We minimise collection, use technical and organisational safeguards, do not sell personal data and require operational providers to act under SautiLink's instructions and standards.

Will SautiLink be free to use?

Yes. The core SautiLink social-network experience is intended to be free, with no subscription required. Your internet or mobile-data provider may still charge its normal connection fees.

What can I use while SautiLink is in pre-launch?

You can use SautiLink Cloud Engine today for DNS, SEO, website security and diagnostic tools. SautiNote is also available for independent journalism and African storytelling.

How do I contact the team?

Use the secure contact form, email support@sautilink.com for support, or email team@sautilink.com for general inquiries.
