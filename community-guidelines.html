SautiLink Community Guidelines

Where these Guidelines apply

These Community Guidelines apply to profiles, usernames, bios, posts, photos, videos, polls, comments, replies, reposts, quotes, Rooms, direct messages, notifications, verification submissions and other member activity on SautiLink.

Room owners and moderators can set additional local rules, but local rules cannot authorize conduct that violates SautiLink-wide safety requirements. Private or audience-restricted spaces are not exempt from these Guidelines.

These Guidelines work alongside the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Upload Guidelines.

Open expression and peaceful disagreement

SautiLink permits criticism, journalism, investigation, political discussion, advocacy, parody, satire, religious and non-religious viewpoints, artistic expression, peaceful organizing and disagreement about public issues. Criticism of a government, political party, public institution, company, ideology, region or public figure is not prohibited merely because it is harsh or unpopular.

You may challenge claims, quote public statements, document misconduct, discuss historical conflict and argue for lawful political or social change. You may also criticize SautiLink itself.

We moderate conduct and risk, not political loyalty. SautiLink does not require members to support a party, politician, government, region, ideology or official position in order to participate.

Political and regional discussion receives stricter safety review

Political and regional speech can affect public safety and social trust quickly. We therefore apply heightened review to content involving elections, government authority, political movements, regional identity, ethnicity, tribe, nationality, borders, communal conflict and calls for collective action.

Allowed

  • supporting or opposing a candidate, party, policy, government decision or political movement;
  • criticizing leaders, institutions, regions or public policy, including strongly worded criticism;
  • peaceful advocacy, protest discussion, journalism, satire, historical analysis and public-interest reporting;
  • discussing allegations or disputed political claims when presented as claims, commentary, reporting or debate rather than deceptive official instructions;
  • regional pride, cultural identity and debate about representation, resources, development or governance.

Not allowed

  • credible threats, instructions or encouragement to attack political opponents, officials, journalists, protesters, voters or communities;
  • content that praises, coordinates or materially assists political or communal violence;
  • intimidation intended to stop people from lawful civic participation, reporting, voting, peaceful assembly or access to essential public services;
  • knowingly deceptive content presented as an official civic instruction when it could materially interfere with participation, such as false dates, locations, eligibility instructions or fabricated government notices;
  • impersonating an election body, public authority, political organization or public official in a way likely to deceive people about official communication;
  • coordinated fake-account activity, artificial engagement or undisclosed network behavior intended to manipulate political or regional conversation;
  • publishing private information to expose political opponents, activists, journalists, officials or community members to harassment or physical danger;
  • calling for exclusion, expulsion, collective punishment, segregation, dehumanization or violence against people because of region, ethnicity, tribe, nationality, race, religion or another protected identity.

We may act faster or apply stronger restrictions where political or regional content creates a credible risk of offline harm, coordinated intimidation or communal violence. Context, intent, reach, target, immediacy and public-interest value can affect the response.

Violence, threats and serious wrongdoing

Do not threaten, encourage, celebrate or coordinate serious physical harm. This includes credible threats, targeted incitement, instructions designed to enable an imminent violent attack, trafficking, kidnapping, extortion, terrorism support or recruitment, and coordination of violent criminal activity.

Documentary, journalistic, educational or condemnatory discussion of violence may be allowed when the context does not promote or facilitate harm. Graphic material may be limited in visibility or removed when necessary to protect people or comply with law.

Hate, harassment and targeted abuse

Disagreement is allowed. Persistent targeted abuse designed to intimidate, silence or endanger someone is not. We can act on stalking, repeated unwanted targeting, sexual harassment, threats, coordinated pile-ons, malicious exposure of private information and severe bullying.

Do not attack or dehumanize people because of race, ethnicity, tribe, nationality, regional origin, religion, sex, gender, disability or another protected characteristic. Do not call for violence, exclusion or collective punishment against a protected group.

Criticism of beliefs, institutions, governments, ideologies, political parties or public conduct is generally allowed when it targets ideas or actions rather than calling for harm against people because of who they are.

Identity, impersonation and verification

Verification badge meanings. A blue checkmark (white in some themes) identifies a verified individual, including a public figure or politician. The orange/pink checkmark identifies a business, brand, government body, organisation or other entity whose public identity SautiLink Corporation has reviewed. It does not mean the account is SautiLink staff, endorse its content, or by itself grant Marketplace merchant certification.

You may use a public name, creator identity, brand identity or pseudonym where the account does not deceptively impersonate another person or organization. Do not create accounts designed to mislead people about who is speaking, especially when pretending to be SautiLink staff, a public authority, journalist, business, political organization or public figure.

Verification is an identity and authenticity signal, not an endorsement of a member's opinions, politics, products or conduct. Verified members must follow the same Community Guidelines and can lose verification when eligibility is lost or serious violations occur.

To apply, open Settings → Account → Verification. The official SautiLink Verification page explains how to get verified, what public evidence can help and the optional public ownership signal. Verification requests are usually reviewed within 14 days, and a rejected request can be subject to the current reapplication period shown in the product.

Spam, manipulation, scams and fraud

Do not use SautiLink to run phishing, credential theft, impersonation scams, deceptive fundraising, fraudulent sales, advance-fee schemes, malicious investment claims or other conduct intended to obtain money, accounts or sensitive information through deception.

Do not operate coordinated fake identities, bot-like spam, repetitive unsolicited messaging, artificial likes/follows/reposts, engagement rings or deceptive networks intended to manipulate ranking, popularity, political discussion or public perception.

Normal promotion, campaigning, community organizing and coordinated lawful advocacy are not prohibited merely because multiple people share the same message. The violation is deceptive or abusive coordination, not genuine collective participation.

Personal information and intimate privacy

Do not publish highly sensitive personal information to facilitate harassment, theft, stalking or physical harm. This can include private home addresses, private phone numbers, passwords, authentication codes, private financial credentials, identity documents or precise real-time location when disclosure creates a serious safety risk.

Do not share non-consensual intimate images, sexual deepfakes or private sexual material without the depicted person's permission. Report urgent privacy or account-safety concerns through the available reporting tools or contact support. See the Privacy Policy for SautiLink data practices.

Child safety and sexual exploitation

SautiLink has zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation, grooming, sextortion, sexualization of minors or sexual abuse material involving children. We may remove content, restrict accounts, preserve necessary evidence and make legally required reports where applicable.

Sexual coercion, trafficking, non-consensual sexual content and threats to share intimate material are prohibited. Explicit sexual content or solicitation can also be restricted where it is incompatible with the feature, audience safety or applicable law. Health, breastfeeding, educational, documentary and artistic context may be treated differently when it is not exploitative.

Self-harm, suicide and dangerous acts

People may talk about mental health, recovery, lived experience and seeking support. Do not encourage, pressure or celebrate suicide or self-harm, target a vulnerable person with encouragement to harm themselves, or use SautiLink to coordinate dangerous acts likely to cause serious injury.

Graphic or instructional material may be restricted when it creates a meaningful risk of imitation or immediate harm. Context such as prevention, journalism, education and recovery support matters.

Malware, cyber abuse and illegal transactions

Do not use SautiLink to distribute malware, steal credentials, coordinate unauthorized access, attack systems, bypass security controls, disrupt services or provide material assistance clearly intended for unlawful cyber abuse.

Do not use the platform to facilitate trafficking, exploitation, unlawful weapons transactions, illegal drugs, stolen goods or other prohibited transactions. Discussion, reporting, harm-reduction information, policy debate and lawful educational content can be treated differently from a transaction or operational facilitation.

Copyright, trademarks and content ownership

Upload or share content only when you own it, have permission, or otherwise have a lawful basis to use it. Do not use SautiLink to knowingly distribute infringing material or impersonate a brand in a deceptive way.

Copyright and intellectual-property complaints can be sent through SautiLink support with enough information to identify the work, the disputed material and the complainant's authority to act.

Posts, comments, Rooms and Messages

Posts, comments, replies and reposts

These Guidelines apply to captions, media, polls, comments, replies, quotes, repost commentary, hashtags and mentions. A repost does not automatically mean endorsement, but adding praise, instructions or targeted harassment can create responsibility for the added context.

Rooms

Rooms may set additional community rules and use Owner, Admin or Moderator roles. Room staff may manage membership and local activity, but cannot override SautiLink-wide rules. Public and private Rooms are both covered.

Direct messages

Messages are intended for conversation participants rather than the public feed, but private messaging is not a loophole for threats, harassment, scams, sexual exploitation, illegal transactions or malicious files. Recipients can report eligible messages or conversations.

Safety and technical requirements for uploads

Media and files must follow these Community Guidelines and the current Upload Guidelines. File type, size, duration, dimensions, attachment count and validation rules are documented there.

A technically valid upload can still be removed if the content violates safety rules. A lawful and safe file can still fail if it exceeds a technical upload limit or is corrupt.

How SautiLink responds to violations

Our response depends on severity, context, likelihood of harm, account history, target, reach and whether a less restrictive action can address the risk. Actions can include:

  • no action when content does not violate the rules;
  • removing or limiting visibility of content;
  • warning the member or limiting a feature;
  • restricting messaging, posting, discovery or other capabilities;
  • removing verification or another authenticity signal when eligibility or integrity requirements are no longer met;
  • suspending or terminating an account for serious or repeated violations;
  • preserving limited evidence where necessary for safety, fraud prevention, appeals or legal obligations.

Not every controversial or reported post will be removed. Reports are signals for review; they are not votes that automatically determine an outcome.

Moderation does not cancel your account privacy controls. You can review the Account & Data Deletion process even if an account is restricted, subject to limited lawful retention of safety or legal records.

Reporting and appeals

Use the Report option available from eligible posts, comments, profiles or conversations when you believe something violates these Guidelines. See how reporting, blocking and muting work.

If SautiLink takes an eligible moderation action against your content or account, you can use the Appeals area to request review. Appeals can result in the original action being upheld or reversed. Additional context, public interest and the applicable rule may be considered.

Do not submit knowingly false reports, mass-report someone merely because you disagree with them, or coordinate reporting to silence lawful political, regional or personal expression.

Frequently asked questions

Does SautiLink allow political opinions and criticism?

Yes. Peaceful political opinions, criticism, reporting, debate, satire and advocacy are allowed. We become stricter when political or regional content involves threats, incitement, intimidation, dangerous deceptive civic instructions, impersonation, coordinated manipulation, doxxing or hate against communities.

How do I get verified on SautiLink?

Read the official SautiLink Verification page, then open Settings → Account → Verification to request verification or check your status.

How do I report something?

Open the available More/Safety menu on the relevant post, comment, profile or conversation and choose Report. More guidance is available in the Help Centre.

How do I appeal a moderation decision?

Open Appeals while signed in. Eligible actions can be reviewed and may be upheld or reversed.
