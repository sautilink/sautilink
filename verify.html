How to get verified on SautiLink.

Request verification from Settings → Account → Verification. Share the requested public evidence, explain your application and follow its progress in the same place.

Apply from your SautiLink account.

Verification is reviewed from the information and public evidence you submit. A badge is an identity signal. It confirms that SautiLink applied its verification process; it is not an endorsement of your opinions, politics, products or conduct.

  1. Open Verification settings. Sign in, open Settings, choose Account and find Verification. If you are eligible to submit, the Request verification action will be available.
  2. Add public evidence. Complete the requested identity details and add at least one supported social profile or article link. Explain the reason for verification clearly and accurately.
  3. Submit and track your status. Return to Settings → Account → Verification for updates. Reviews are usually completed within 14 days. A rejected request may have a reapplication period shown in the product.

Add an optional public ownership signal.

This gives reviewers another way to connect your SautiLink application with an account you listed. It is not required when you have other acceptable public evidence. Use the link only on an account you control.

  1. List your social profile. In the verification application, add the username of a supported social account you control.
  2. Add the SautiLink link. Place sautilink.com/verify in that account’s Bio, About or Links section, depending on the service.
  3. Confirm it in your application. Tick the optional confirmation in SautiLink. Requests with a clear ownership signal may be easier to review and may receive queue priority.

An ownership link is not a guarantee of verification.

Verification badge meanings. A blue checkmark (white in some themes) identifies a verified individual, including a public figure or politician. The orange/pink checkmark identifies a business, brand, government body, organisation or other entity whose public identity SautiLink Corporation has reviewed. It does not mean the account is SautiLink staff, endorse its content, or by itself grant Marketplace merchant certification.

Choose an account type when applying: Individual, Business, Brand, Entity or Organisation. Individuals can provide a public social profile or news/blog article under the normal verification rules. Businesses, brands, entities and organisations must include at least one independent news or blog article about the entity, with up to five article links. Social profiles can support those applications but cannot replace the required articles. This profile-verification form does not ask for ID or business-licence uploads. Marketplace merchant certification follows its separate requirements.

Adding this link does not automatically qualify an account for a badge and does not replace the other evidence in your application. SautiLink still reviews the account, public identity and supporting information under its verification criteria.

The ownership signal is optional. If you do not control another social account, you can still apply using other acceptable public evidence.

Keep your account credentials private.

Never share passwords, login codes, recovery codes or private account credentials with SautiLink as part of verification.

Follow your application.

Return to Settings → Account → Verification for the latest status. Reviews are usually completed within 14 days. Also watch your email, including your spam folder, for updates.
