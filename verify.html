Official verification guide

How to get verified on SautiLink.

Signed-in members can request verification from Settings → Account → Verification. Submit the requested public evidence, explain why the account should be verified, and return to the same section to track the review.

Official destinationSettings → Account → Verification
Typical reviewWithin 14 days
Badge meansIdentity signal
Apply

Request verification from your SautiLink account.

Verification is reviewed from the information and public evidence you submit. A badge confirms that SautiLink applied its verification process; it is not an endorsement of your opinions, politics, products or conduct.

01

Open Verification settings

Sign in, open Settings, choose Account and find Verification. If you are eligible to submit, the Request verification action will be available.

02

Add public evidence

Complete the requested identity details and add at least one supported social profile or article link. Explain the reason for verification clearly and accurately.

03

Submit and track status

After submission, return to Settings → Account → Verification for updates. Reviews are usually completed within 14 days. A rejected request may have a reapplication period shown in the product.

How it works

Use the link only on an account you control.

This optional public ownership signal gives reviewers another way to connect the SautiLink application with an account you listed. It is not required when you have other acceptable public evidence.

01

List your social profile

In the verification application, add the username of a supported social account you control.

02

Add the SautiLink link

Place sautilink.com/verify in that account's Bio, About or Links section, depending on the service.

03

Confirm it in your application

Tick the optional confirmation in SautiLink. Requests with a clear ownership signal may be easier to review and may receive queue priority.

Important

This is not a guarantee of verification.

Adding this link does not automatically qualify an account for a badge and does not replace the other evidence in your application. SautiLink still reviews the account, public identity and supporting information under its verification criteria.

The ownership signal is optional. If you do not control another social account, you can still apply using other acceptable public evidence.

Never share passwords, login codes, recovery codes or private account credentials with SautiLink as part of verification.
