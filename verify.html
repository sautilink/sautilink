Account verification

A simple public signal can help us confirm account ownership.

If you are applying for a SautiLink verification badge and listed another social profile, you may voluntarily place sautilink.com/verify in that profile's Bio, About or Links section while your request is being reviewed.

How it works

Use the link only on an account you control.

The signal gives reviewers another public way to connect the SautiLink application with the external account you listed.

01

List your social profile

In the verification application, add the username of a supported social account you control.

02

Add the SautiLink link

Place sautilink.com/verify in that account's Bio, About or Links section, depending on the service.

03

Confirm it in your application

Tick the optional confirmation in SautiLink. Requests with a clear ownership signal may be easier to review and may receive queue priority.

Important

This is not a guarantee of verification.

Adding this link does not automatically qualify an account for a badge and does not replace the other evidence in your application. SautiLink still reviews the account, public identity and supporting information under its verification criteria.

The ownership signal is optional. If you do not control another social account, you can still apply using other acceptable public evidence.

Never share passwords, login codes, recovery codes or private account credentials with SautiLink as part of verification.
