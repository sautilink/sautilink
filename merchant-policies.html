Access and eligibility

The SautiLink Marketplace is available only in the SautiLink app. These policies govern merchant eligibility, product listings and selling activity, together with our Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy and Upload Guidelines.

Only merchants who complete the applicable SautiLink verification route may list or sell products. Businesses and companies must be verified and certified by SautiLink Corporation before they can sell. Unverified or otherwise ineligible merchants are not permitted to list or sell through Marketplace.

Small or startup entrepreneurs without their own office have a separate route through normal account verification, explained in the small-entrepreneur exception. A Room name or category called “Marketplace” does not grant merchant status or replace these requirements.

Verification for Businesses

For Verification for Businesses, an authorized owner or representative must submit accurate, current details and genuine documents they are entitled to provide.

Business identity
Business or company name, and relevant ownership or authorized-representative information.
Location and contact
The physical business location and a working contact phone number.
Taxpayer Identification Number
Your TIN and supporting TIN documentation for the business verification application.
VAT information Optional
VAT details are optional for this application. Information supplied must be accurate; your own tax obligations still apply.
Proof of lawful operation
Applicable BRELA registration records, a business licence, TIN records, or other licences and certificates relevant to the lawful operation of your business. Supply the documents applicable to your business; a TIN does not replace a required business licence.
Relevant supporting details
Other information reasonably necessary to confirm identity, authority to apply, location and eligibility. We may request clarification or updated evidence.
  1. Submit your application.Complete the requested business details and supporting documents.
  2. Document review.SautiLink reviews the submission and seeks clarification where needed.
  3. Business-site verification.SautiLink visits the stated business location to verify the premises and the information supplied.
  4. Approval and certification.Following successful verification, SautiLink Corporation issues a special merchant verification checkmark and a platform merchant certificate.

Submitting an application is not approval. Do not sell as a certified business before the process is successfully completed. There is no guaranteed approval or fixed review time.

Small and startup entrepreneurs

No office of your own? Small or startup entrepreneurs without their own office are an exception to the formal business verification route.

These entrepreneurs must complete the normal blue tick verification process through Settings → Account → Verification in the SautiLink app. They do not need to present an office for the business-site visit or complete the formal business-document route described above in the same way.

Once normal verification is approved, an eligible small or startup entrepreneur can use this exception to sell under these Merchant Policies. They are not represented as a SautiLink-certified company merely because they hold a standard blue tick. A company cannot use an ordinary account badge to bypass business verification and certification.

This is an exception to SautiLink’s business-premises and verification process, not an exemption from laws, tax obligations, product-safety duties or other rules applicable to the seller. Honest listings, customer responsibilities and all platform rules apply to both routes.

Free services and Marketplace listings

All SautiLink services are free. SautiLink charges no registration, application, verification, site-visit, certification, merchant-checkmark, Marketplace-access or listing fees, and no sales commission.

Do not pay anyone who claims that a payment to SautiLink is needed to approve, accelerate or maintain your verification or certificate. Report such requests to support@sautilink.com.

Marketplace product listings are separate from advertisements. SautiLink does not currently offer an advertising service. A Marketplace listing is not a purchased advertisement, paid placement or paid boost.

Free platform services do not mean that merchants’ products are free. Sellers must clearly state their product prices and any agreed delivery or other transaction charges. Taxes, statutory registration or licensing costs, internet charges and independent third-party charges are not fees charged by SautiLink.

What a checkmark and certificate mean

A special merchant checkmark and platform certificate indicate successful completion of SautiLink Corporation’s own business verification process. A standard blue tick under the small-entrepreneur exception indicates normal account verification.

These signals do not replace government registration, a tax obligation, a business licence or any legally required authorization. They are not a guarantee of every product, transaction, delivery, refund or merchant’s future conduct, and must not be advertised as government approval or a product warranty.

Use only the status issued to your own account. Do not forge, sell, transfer, lend or alter a certificate or checkmark, or use it to misrepresent another business. Keep business names, location, contacts, documents and eligibility current, and notify SautiLink of material changes. We may reassess merchant status when information changes or safety concerns arise.

Accurate and honest product listings

  • Describe the product truthfully, including its price, condition, availability, material defects, relevant restrictions and what the customer receives.
  • Use photographs and descriptions that fairly represent the actual offer. Upload only material you own, have permission to use or otherwise have a lawful basis to share.
  • Make only claims you can support. Do not invent discounts, stock, reviews, testimonials, qualifications or business affiliations.
  • State any delivery charge or other cost the customer must pay before they commit. Correct or remove inaccurate, unavailable or unlawful offers.
  • Do not impersonate merchants, misuse SautiLink branding or badges, spam people, manipulate engagement or evade selling restrictions through another account.

Prohibited products and conduct

Do not list or facilitate illegal, stolen, counterfeit, recalled or unsafe products; illegal drugs; prohibited weapons or explosives; trafficking or exploitation; malware, stolen credentials or unlawfully obtained personal information.

Scams, deceptive financial schemes, fraudulent sales, intellectual-property infringement, and products or conduct prohibited by our Community Guidelines are not allowed. Do not misrepresent a regulated product’s approval, safety or lawful uses.

Where a product is legally restricted, the seller must hold the necessary permissions and comply with applicable restrictions, including any age or distribution limits. SautiLink may remove or restrict a listing where legality, authenticity or safety cannot be established. Verification does not make a prohibited product permissible.

Your customers and your responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for lawful trading, applicable registrations and licences, taxes, product safety, truthful claims and the promises they make to customers. Agree clearly on the price, fulfilment and delivery arrangements, and applicable cancellation, return, refund and warranty terms before the customer commits.

Honour those obligations and mandatory consumer rights. Respond fairly to complaints, correct errors and provide remedies required by law. Do not threaten customers, suppress honest complaints or ask anyone for passwords, one-time login codes or recovery credentials.

SautiLink’s verification and certification do not make SautiLink the seller, a party to a merchant’s sale, or a guarantor of its outcome. Nothing in these policies excludes consumer rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Business information and privacy

For merchant verification, SautiLink processes the business name, location, phone number, TIN, optional VAT details, supporting registration or licensing documents, relevant verification communications and findings from the business-site visit. We use this information to assess eligibility, verify the business, prevent fraud, administer merchant status and address safety or legal issues, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Provide only relevant information you are authorized to submit. Do not put identity documents, tax documents or other private verification evidence in public listings. A business contact or location you choose to publish is different from private application evidence.

Protect customer information and use it only for legitimate, disclosed purposes connected with the transaction and applicable legal obligations. Do not sell customer data or use it for unrelated contact without a lawful basis. Privacy requests and questions can be sent to support@sautilink.com.

Reports, enforcement and review

Report suspected scams, misleading listings, unsafe products or misuse of merchant status through available reporting tools or support@sautilink.com. Keep relevant records and avoid sharing sensitive evidence publicly.

SautiLink may request corrections or further evidence, reject an application, remove listings, restrict selling access, withdraw a merchant certificate or checkmark, or take account action under its Terms and Community Guidelines. Serious fraud, urgent safety concerns or legal obligations may require immediate action.

To request review of a merchant-verification or certification decision, contact support with the decision and relevant information. Eligible content or account decisions can use the available appeals process. Review does not guarantee approval or reversal, and an account must not bypass restrictions while a review is pending.

Updates and contact

These Merchant Policies take effect on October 20, 2026. We may update them as Marketplace rules, safety needs or legal requirements change. The effective date identifies the current version; where a change materially affects your rights or responsibilities, we will provide appropriate notice as described in our Terms.

For merchant-policy questions, verification concerns or suspected fee requests, contact support@sautilink.com or visit Contact SautiLink. For privacy matters, use support@sautilink.com.
