02 · When accounts launch

Delete a SautiLink account

Every SautiLink app that allows account creation will also let the account owner initiate permanent deletion inside the app. The expected route is Settings → Account → Delete Account. This web page will also provide a secure authenticated deletion route before public accounts become available.

  1. Open the deletion control and review what will be removed.
  2. Reauthenticate or enter a code sent to an existing verified contact method.
  3. Confirm permanent deletion. We will not require a phone call, a subscription cancellation or an explanation.
  4. Access is deactivated promptly and deletion processing begins.
  5. We confirm completion within the stated timeline.

Temporary deactivation, muting notifications or removing an app from a device does not delete an account. The deletion control will clearly distinguish those choices.

03 · Scope

What permanent deletion covers

After a verified account-deletion request, SautiLink will delete or irreversibly anonymize data associated with the account from active systems, including:

  • account credentials, profile details, settings and linked verification details;
  • posts, replies, media uploads, reactions, saved items and drafts;
  • direct-message content associated with the deleting account, subject to the rights and copies of other conversation participants;
  • follows, blocks, mutes, notification choices and personalization history; and
  • eligible support, device and activity records that no longer need to be retained.

Content copied, quoted or reshared by another person, captured in a screenshot, indexed outside SautiLink, or lawfully published elsewhere may remain beyond our control. We will remove the deleting user's original content from active SautiLink surfaces as required by our policy and applicable law.

04 · Exceptions

What may be retained for a limited time

Deletion from active systems is targeted for completion within 30 days. Encrypted backup copies may remain for up to 90 days before routine overwrite and will not be returned to active use.

We may retain the minimum data necessary when required by law, to complete an active safety or fraud investigation, preserve evidence of serious abuse, establish or defend legal claims, or demonstrate that a deletion request was completed. Retained records are restricted and are not used for ordinary product features, recommendations or marketing.

Account under restriction? A user may still request deletion. We may preserve limited enforcement evidence to prevent repeated severe abuse, but we will not keep unrelated account data merely because an account was suspended.

05 · Support

Deletion help and account safety

If you cannot access the email or device associated with an account, contact support. We will use a proportionate identity-verification process designed to prevent someone else from deleting your account. Never send passwords, one-time codes or full identity documents unless an official, clearly explained recovery process specifically requires limited evidence.
