# SautiLink legal policy update — 2026-09-09

This checkpoint records the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service refresh for the live SautiLink social network.

## Product state reflected

- live accounts and profile controls;
- posts, media, polls, replies, conversations and saved content;
- follows, notifications and discovery;
- direct messages and realtime messaging;
- Rooms (user-facing name) with public/private membership, roles and moderation;
- reporting, moderation and appeals;
- account verification and identity controls;
- account export/deletion controls;
- Google, Facebook and Microsoft sign-in;
- Supabase and Cloudflare infrastructure;
- Ivy Network PLC identified as SautiLink's technology/infrastructure partner without assigning it unrestricted data rights or blanket legal responsibility.

## Drafting approach

The update uses original SautiLink wording and borrows only general legal-policy structure and common platform concepts from large social-network policies. It intentionally avoids copying provider language verbatim.

The legal pages no longer describe SautiLink as a pre-launch waitlist product. Strong promises that could unnecessarily lock future product decisions were also removed, including a permanent guarantee that every core feature will always remain free.

The Privacy Policy does not claim that current direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. It explains that message content can be processed to deliver, secure and moderate the service.

## UI scope

`assets/legal-policy.css` is scoped to `body.legal-policy-page`, so the flatter document-first legal layout applies only to `/privacy` and `/terms`. Existing pages such as Account Deletion keep their current layout.
